# Semanto by Trooth for Shopify: App Store listing copy

Plain words, Trooth's house words. No number Trooth did not measure, no outcome promised (requirements 4.3.3 and 4.4.1, Built for Shopify 4.3.1), no pricing outside the pricing section (4.2), no reviews or testimonials (4.3.7). Each field is within Shopify's limit.

## App name (30 characters at most)

Semanto by Trooth

## Subtitle (62 characters at most; a sentence, not keywords)

A signed copy of your products that AI tools can check

## Introduction (100 characters at most)

Keep a signed, current copy of your store's products with Trooth for AI assistants and search tools.

## Details (500 characters at most)

Semanto by Trooth connects your store to your company on Trooth. You approve the connection on trooth.co; no password or key is typed here. The app sends your active products, prices and stock to Trooth now and each time a product changes; Trooth records them as declared by your company and signs them with your company's key. An app embed adds two tags to your pages that point AI assistants and search tools to that signed copy, so anyone can check a copy against what your store says.

## Key features (3 to 5, 80 characters each at most)

- Connect to your Trooth company in one approval on trooth.co
- Sends products, prices and stock once, then each change as you make it
- App embed adds two head tags pointing readers to your signed copy
- Reads products only; never customers or orders
- Disconnect any time; the store's key is revoked at once

## Demo screencast script (English, 75 seconds, unlisted YouTube)

1. Open the app from Apps in a development store. The Welcome step says what the app does.
2. Press Get started, then Connect to Trooth. A trooth.co window opens; sign in, choose the company, press Approve. Back in Shopify the step moves on by itself.
3. Press Turn on app embed. The theme editor opens on App embeds; switch on Semanto by Trooth and press Save. Back in the app the status reads On.
4. The First sync step shows each step finish: read from Shopify, prepare, send to Trooth, Trooth's receipt.
5. Done: open the Semanto dashboard. Edit a product's price in Shopify; Settings shows the new last sync.
6. Settings: Disconnect, confirm in the modal; the status reads Not connected.

## Testing instructions for the reviewer (numbered, with values)

1. Install the app on the review store from the listing. It opens on the Welcome step.
2. Press Get started, then Connect to Trooth. In the trooth.co window, sign in with the review account given in the submission form (a Trooth company named "Shopify Review Co" is ready in it) and press Approve.
3. Return to the Shopify tab. Within a few seconds the step changes to Turn on the app embed.
4. Press Turn on app embed, switch on Semanto by Trooth in the theme editor, press Save, and come back. Status: On.
5. The First sync step finishes on its own (the review store has five products). The home page then shows Semanto is set up.
6. Change any product's price. Open Settings: Last sync shows the time of the change.
7. In Settings press Disconnect, then Disconnect in the confirmation. Status: Not connected.
