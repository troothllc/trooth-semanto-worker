Shopify's listing image rules (requirements 4.4.3 to 4.4.5, enforced from 26 March 2026): each image shows the app's real screens, each is different, no desktop background or browser window, no logo-only image, no pricing text, no reviews, no Shopify trademark misuse, no number Trooth did not measure.

## The six screenshots, in order

1. **Welcome.** The setup guide at step 1: the step badges (Welcome current), the three plain-word lines of what the app does, "It reads products only. It never reads customers or orders.", and the Get started button. Aside: What Semanto does.
2. **Connect to Trooth, waiting.** The info banner "Waiting for your approval on trooth.co" with the time left, the spinner and Open Trooth again. Shows that no password or key is typed in Shopify.
3. **Turn on the app embed.** The three numbered instructions, Status: Off, and the Turn on app embed button.
4. **Theme editor, App embeds tab.** The theme editor opened by the deep link with Semanto by Trooth switched on. Crop to the editor's App embeds panel (Shopify's own screen, so it carries no claim of ours).
5. **First sync.** The four steps: Read products from Shopify (Done, "5 products read" style detail), Prepare products for Trooth (Done), Send to Trooth (In progress, spinner), Trooth's receipt (Waiting).
6. **Settings.** Connection to Trooth: Connected, Trooth ID sample-co, connected date, store key id, Open the Semanto dashboard and Disconnect; Product sync with the last sync and its finished steps; App embed: On; Recent activity.
